1. ABOUT THE DATASET
------------

Title:	Characterization and Evaluation data from the National Fruit Collection 2025_Apple

Creator(s): Matthew Ordidge[1] (https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0115-5218), Penny Hale [2]

Organisation(s): 1. University of Reading. 2. Fruit Advisory Services Team Llp

Rights-holder(s): Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs

Publication Year: 2025

Description: Data are characterization and evaluation scores and measures collected during the curation of the National Fruit Collection (apple collection). Data were largely collected in line with the published ECPGR Characterization and Evaluation Descriptors for Apple Genetic Resources with additional locally agreed descriptors.  The dataset contains scores across 25 traits from 2166 accessions.

4. CONTENTS
------------
File listing

NFC_C_E_data_Apple_21_08_25.csv

Data are presented in the form of a .csv file and represent characterization and evaluation descriptor scores for accessions in the National Fruit Collection (past and present).

Columns 1-12 contain MUNQ code (Denancé C, Muranty H, Durel C-E (2020) MUNQ—Malus UNiQue genotype code for grouping apple accessions corresponding to a unique genotypic profile, V1 edn. Portail Data INRAE. https://doi.org/10.15454/HKGMAS), Accession numbers, Accession names, Cultivar type, Row, Tree and Collection location data (for existing and prior collections) as well as notes on whether accessions are current and/or whether data are available in the EURISCO database. Columns 13, 42, 43 and 45 contain details on the data source (including name of recorder where available) and both dates of sample harvest and recording in relation to the scores in subsequent columns. Columns 39-40 detail the position of the tree used for subsequent description. Descriptors are generally presented in both written descriptive and numerical rated format.


5. METHODS
-----------

Data were generally scored following protocols published by ECPGR (https://www.ecpgr.org/resources/ecpgr-publications/publication/ecpgr-characterization-and-evaluation-descriptors-for-apple-genetic-resources-2022 accessed 22/08/25). Additional 'local' descriptors were added to categorise 'Standardised recording date' and floweing time (based on an average of ten years of measurement). Data were collected over a period of time (approx 2000-2024) with sampling as indicated in the file. Data cited to Smith in column 13 are standardised from descriptions published in the UK National Apple Register (Smith MWG (1971) National apple register of the United Kingdom. Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, London). As described in the register, these included a combination of data from the collection and verified published descriptions where data were not available at the time. The location of the collection is 51.29979, 0.87834.
Where the scorer's judgement was that more than one category was represented, data have been standardised to the main or median state. Data collectors (P.Hale) are detailed in the file where these are recorded, data have been collated by M Ordidge (as above).
